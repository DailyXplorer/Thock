# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below doesn't replace it.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Thock to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@DailyXplorer](https://github.com/DailyXplorer), through a [private report](https://github.com/DailyXplorer/thock/security/advisories/new), or use GitHub's "Report content" option. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or ban, temporarily or for good, anyone who breaks this code.
