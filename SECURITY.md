# Security policy

Thock listens to every keystroke, so security reports matter. Thank you for taking the time.

## Supported versions

Only the latest commit on `main` gets fixes.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/DailyXplorer/thock/security/advisories/new) (Security tab > Advisories).

Please include:

- what an attacker can do, and under which conditions;
- steps or a proof of concept to reproduce it;
- macOS version, Mac model and the Thock commit you tested.

You should get a first answer within a week. Once a fix is ready, the advisory is published with credit to you, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: anything that lets Thock or a crafted sound pack read, store or send what you type, escape the App Sandbox, crash or hang the app from imported files, or reach the network.

Out of scope: issues that require an attacker who already controls your user account, and macOS behavior Thock can't change, such as secure input or Input Monitoring itself.
